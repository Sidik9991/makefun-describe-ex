## The piece

> Expired

## How it was chosen

Six candidates (Blocked, Mistake, Overdraft, Unstable, Expired, Trial) were scored against the judge's five criteria in order, with criterion 1 — instantly readable as a verdict on a former partner — weighted double as the gate. "Expired" won: the whole relationship as a date on a carton — fine once, stayed too long, and keeping it now would only make you sick. A twist instead of a plain insult, and a nod from anyone who has cleaned out the fridge after a breakup.

`main.py` holds the candidates and scores, validates the winning word against the gate (one word, 1–111 characters), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
